October 3–4, 2026 Chalmers, Gothenburg

Hack Chalmers is a 24-hour hackathon in Gothenburg.

Come with an idea, build something from scratch, and show everyone what you made at the end.

No theme. No tracks. Just build something cool.

When & Where

October 3–4, 2026
SB-G216, Samhällsbyggnad
Sven Hultins gata 6, Chalmers Johanneberg

24 hours, Saturday to Sunday.

The room closes at midnight, so we head home for the night and pick it back up at 08:00 on Sunday.

How it works / Agenda

Saturday

  • 13:30 — Check-in & opening
  • 14:00 — Hacking starts
  • Evening — Food and building
  • 00:00 — Room closes, head home

Sunday

  • 08:00 — Room opens, back at it
  • 10:45 — Hacking ends
  • 11:00–11:45 — Demos & judging
  • 11:45 — Winners announced

Exact schedule may change.

Judging

We're keeping it simple: how cool is the thing you built?

That's basically it.

Solo or team, both work. If you come alone we'll help you find a team at the start.

Prizes

The winning team gets $2,500 in Prelint credits. Every team gets $250 to use during the build.

Everyone on the winning team also gets lifetime premium on KöKoll, worth 1 990 kr each.
