Hack
Chalmers

A 24-hour hackathon in Gothenburg.
Build something great.

October 3–4, 2026 Chalmers, Gothenburg

No theme. No tracks.

Come with an idea, build something from scratch, and show everyone what you made at the end. Bring a project you've wanted to make, or turn up alone and find people to make something with.

Twenty-four hours, Saturday noon to Sunday noon. Nothing pre-built, nothing off the shelf.

When & where

October 3–4, 2026. Saturday 12:00 to Sunday 12:00.

Chalmers University of Technology
